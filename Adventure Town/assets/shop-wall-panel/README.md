# shop-wall-panel

Create one reusable low cutaway shop wall segment, width about three times its height, shallow rectangular depth. Warm cream plaster inset framed by honey-brown timber upright end posts and a continuous timber top cap and baseboard, a few large cubic plaster irregularities. Solid rectangular ends designed to meet another segment, simple straight silhouette. It is a freestanding single low wall module, not a room, no door or window or attached furniture. Camera slightly above to see shallow thickness. Intended to improve existing 0.48-unit cutaway walls without obstructing gameplay.

Status: **ready-for-test-drive**. Original PNG and PLY are committed and verified. Repeated low plaster/timber panels in the grocery and potion shop. Conversion settings and the individual rig, where applicable, are fitted to that actual PLY. See [runtime review](runtime/REVIEW.md) and [report](runtime/report.json). Runtime binaries are delivered separately for user extraction and commit.

Low-resolution decoration on an authored wall backing; joints and timber trim are authored. No room geometry or collision is inferred from the splat.
