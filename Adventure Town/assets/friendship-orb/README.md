# friendship-orb

Create one small opaque magical friendship orb as a single solid connected voxel object: stepped spherical pale ivory shell, a warm pink equatorial band and one large inset heart-shaped rosy emblem facing camera, modest pale-gold rim around emblem. A friendly handmade fantasy object with no recognizable brand design. No transparency within the orb, no glow cloud, no particles, no floating pieces, no platform. The surrounding background IS genuinely transparent. Simple readable silhouette and large color areas for voxel reconstruction. Future capture and transformation effects will be authored separately.

Status: **ready-for-test-drive**. Original PNG and PLY are committed and verified. Visible friendship projectile used to catch a ready animal. Conversion settings and the individual rig, where applicable, are fitted to that actual PLY. See [runtime review](runtime/REVIEW.md) and [report](runtime/report.json). Runtime binaries are delivered separately for user extraction and commit.

The original splat has a dark inferred rear; that dark rear remains in the voxel mesh. The heart is clearest from the front. It is ready for interaction testing, with rear-color art review still pending.
