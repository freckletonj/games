# town-bench

Create one cozy public town bench with honey-brown thick wooden seat planks, a simple connected wooden backrest, two sturdy dark timber support legs and short armrests, subtle moss-green painted accents on end supports. Width about 2.4 times total height. No carved lettering, cushions, loose objects, plants, people or ground. Three-quarter almost frontal view shows seat, back and depth; genuine transparent openings between seat/back and beneath seat, no intersecting supports. Chunky connected cubic construction matching a riverside fantasy village.

Status: **ready-for-test-drive**. Original PNG and PLY are committed and verified. Two public benches with separate authored collision. Conversion settings and the individual rig, where applicable, are fitted to that actual PLY. See [runtime review](runtime/REVIEW.md) and [report](runtime/report.json). Runtime binaries are delivered separately for user extraction and commit.

Back and seat edges are reconstructed approximately. The separate authored collision is deliberately simple. No seating animation is implemented.
