# flintfist

Create one very cute small bear-like stone creature called Flintfist, made of soft-looking chunky warm slate-gray cubic stone, cream face and belly, moss-green patches confined to shoulders, tiny rounded ears, large kind dark eyes and a gentle smile, large sturdy empty paws and short strong legs. Friendly childlike proportions, head one third overall height. BIPEDAL neutral symmetric A-pose with arms angled 35 degrees out and hands clear of waist, both legs separate down to broad feet; readable arm and leg joints for a fitted humanoid skeleton. Almost frontal camera turned ten degrees at chest height. No weapons, clothes, accessories or loose rocks. Future role is pushing heavy obstacles.

Status: **ready-for-test-drive**. Original PNG and PLY are committed and verified. Befriendable rock bear and playable push/punch form for the optional ruin obstacles. Conversion settings and the individual rig, where applicable, are fitted to that actual PLY. See [runtime review](runtime/REVIEW.md) and [report](runtime/report.json). Runtime binaries are delivered separately for user extraction and commit.

Broad shoulders and short legs have individually fitted soft weights. Push and punch are modest procedural gestures, not authored contact animation; object contact timing is controlled by gameplay. Small voxel creases and foot drift remain.
