# tidefox

Create one very cute small semi-aquatic fox-like fantasy animal called Tidefox: soft teal coat, pale cream muzzle/chest/belly, small triangular ears, big warm dark eyes, short chunky legs and slightly broad webbed paws, thick tapering otter-like tail curving away from the legs and attached clearly at rear. Friendly curious expression. Neutral standing quadruped rest pose with four separated paws, visible transparent gaps beneath belly and around tail, gentle three-quarter view both eyes visible. No water, clothing, accessories, effects or scenery. Future role is swimming and diving.

Status: **ready-for-test-drive**. Original PNG and PLY are committed and verified. Befriendable aquatic fox and playable faster-swim/dive form. Conversion settings and the individual rig, where applicable, are fitted to that actual PLY. See [runtime review](runtime/REVIEW.md) and [report](runtime/report.json).
